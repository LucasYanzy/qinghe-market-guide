# Interactive analytics demo

The public browser demo starts with an editable catalogue and no generated transactions. University analytics and the vendor analytics page offer **Load historical sample**. Loading is optional and scoped to the current browser tab; it never calls a production seeding API.

The loader adds 12 weeks of dated, synthetic completed/cancelled orders, item snapshots, completed-order receipts, attendance and whole-session sales reports for the original demo vendor. Samples include returning and first-time accounts, classified payment amounts and deliberately unclassified reports. Dates are relative to the Eastern business day when loaded and stay fixed thereafter. Reloads and repeated requests reuse the original sample instead of adding it again or moving history forward.

Existing orders, product edits, stock, market configuration and SNAP acceptance remain intact. Historical samples do not reserve today's stock. User-created sessions are skipped; an error rolls back the entire generation. The sample banner, date window and exported report source distinguish the dataset from real operations. Switching roles shares the same dataset; another independently opened tab starts fresh. The existing Reset confirmation clears all data in the current tab.

Payment shares use classified whole-session sales only; unknown amounts are shown as unknown and explicit zero amounts have no percentage when the denominator is zero. These sales reports include simulated platform receipts and separate walk-up sales, so they must not be added to platform revenue. Historical SNAP examples do not assert current acceptance or real benefit redemption. New interactive orders continue through confirmation, customer pickup-code verification and receipt recording, then update the same analytics.

The payment mix, fulfillment and catalogue charts support donut/bar views, segment clicks, native legend buttons, previous/next category controls and arrow/Home/End keys. Mouse hover temporarily previews a value and its explanation; leaving or pressing Escape restores the selection. Only clicking or keyboard selection applies a catalogue filter. Switching chart style or payment sorting preserves the selection and category colors. On phones the readout precedes the legend and remains visible while scrolling through categories. Chart animations are disabled, with CSS feedback respecting reduced motion.

Overview and report trends offer previous/next point controls, an accessible date slider and a latest-record shortcut. The overview starts at the last day with orders; report trends start at the last recorded value, including explicit zeroes. Missing records remain missing. Each chart renders at most 120 periods and its complete detail table pages 12 rows at a time; CSV exports retain the full reporting range.

University analytics refresh from the current database every 30 seconds while visible, and on returning to the page. Updates can be paused; manual refresh remains available. Hidden pages and other university pages do not poll. Requests have a 15-second timeout and stale responses cannot replace a newer filter. A background failure preserves the last matching snapshot and chart selections, shows the failure and offers retry. Completed order value, unique participants, average order value and period comparisons are recalculated from order records, not fixed sample percentages. Pending or cancelled orders do not add completed value or participants. Comparisons use the immediately preceding equal-length period; a zero baseline has no percentage change. Whole-session sales reports update when a vendor saves a report; they are separate from order receipts.

The public demo continues to use the database in the current browser tab, rather than sharing data across visitors or devices. Sample generation is loaded on demand and stored once, avoiding startup seeding and repeated database exports.
